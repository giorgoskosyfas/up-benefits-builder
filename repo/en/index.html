Frequently asked questions

What is the Up Benefits Builder?

It is a free, 7-question interactive tool that recommends which mix of Up Hellas solutions (FlexOne, Up Gift, Fitpass, Up Expense and Chèque Déjeuner) fits a Greek company, with a short reason for each solution.

Which Up Hellas solution suits hybrid or remote teams?

For hybrid and remote teams the tool usually recommends FlexOne, because it offers digital cards and categories such as WFH and public transport on one card. If wellbeing is a goal, it adds Fitpass, which also includes online training.

What is the difference between FlexOne and Chèque Déjeuner?

FlexOne is one digital and physical card with many benefit categories and an employee app. Chèque Déjeuner is classic meal vouchers, ideal for frontline teams who prefer paying without an app or smartphone.

How is the recommendation made? Is it written by AI?

The solutions are chosen by transparent rules based on your answers. AI only writes a short personalised summary from a closed product catalogue, and it is not allowed to mention prices, amounts or tax limits.

Will I see prices or tax benefits?

No. Pricing, plans and tax benefits depend on your company and are confirmed by an advisor. For the general picture, see the Up Hellas Tax Benefits Guide.

What happens to my details?

Your quiz answers contain no personal data. Your name, email and phone only go to the sales team so they can contact you, and they are never sent to the AI model.
